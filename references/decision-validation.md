# Decision-validation extensions

The skill’s **Route the task** mode 3 (Decision validation)—not step 3 of the 11-step method (Build three evidence layers)—may test a bounded opportunity or positioning hypothesis. The extensions on this page are **experiments**, not default truth. Do not silently fold them into a market-landscape study.

They exist because the core method already supports landscape description, sampling, coding, claim discipline, and commercial-path *existence*. It does not, by itself, license fixed-age causal panels, matched comparisons, independent demand tests, or transaction-level commercial proof.

## What stays unvalidated

### 1. Fixed-age panels (7 day / 14 day)

A panel that re-observes the same posts at a fixed age (classically 7 days and 14 days after publish) would separate content age from cumulative metrics. That design is attractive and still **unvalidated** as a default path.

You may run it as an experiment if the research contract freezes the ages, the recapture rules, and the metric type beforehand. You may not:

- treat likes-per-day as a substitute for a fixed-age panel;
- declare author or format decline from mixed-age cumulative totals;
- promote a 7/14-day panel into the standard workflow just because the numbers look clean.

Core-method guidance still stands: label decline only with comparable fixed-age observations, enough repeated samples, and preferably exposure or completion data — and write “not established” when those are missing.

### 2. Causal matching

Matching posts or authors that look similar except for a treatment (a hotspot, a format, a product mention) is an unvalidated extension. Observational similarity is not a causal design. If you try matching, pre-register covariates, calipers, and the outcome; retain unmatched cases and counterexamples; and still write “associated in this sample” unless the design truly supports causality.

### 3. Demand validation beyond comment coding

Comment demand/topic coding (with kappa gates) describes *visible comment language in the sample*. It does not validate addressable demand, willingness to pay, or that “people will buy this.” Separate demand-validation work — search interest, waitlists, surveys, sales tests — is an unvalidated extension. “想看看” or “收藏了” still does not mean purchased.

### 4. Transaction and commercial proof

`SKILL.md` section 9 separates commercial *path* from commercial *proof*:

1. official platform cooperation capability;
2. visible brand mention or sponsorship label;
3. creator-stated cooperation or case result;
4. verifiable order, revenue, cost, collection, repeat purchase, or contract evidence.

Levels 1–3 remain core-method observations about path existence. Systematically collecting level-4 proof, and using it to estimate conversion, GMV, commission, or profitability, is an unvalidated extension. Never estimate those quantities from unsourced creator claims.

## Five gates before an extension becomes default

An extension may graduate into the default (core) workflow only when **all five** hold:

1. **Independent run.** It has been executed end-to-end on a study other than the method's originating extract, with `raw/`, `analysis/`, and `audit/` retained.
2. **Frozen definitions.** Ages, matching rules, demand tests, or transaction-evidence rules are written into the research contract *before* outcome inspection.
3. **Quality report.** Missingness, personalization risk, join completeness, metric type, and sample-frame limits are audited and disclosed.
4. **Independent review.** Reviewers who receive the same frozen packet challenge method, counterexamples, and commercialization or feasibility claims, citing evidence IDs.
5. **Ledger reconciliation.** Any tension with core-method findings is recorded in the claim ledger. A successful experiment does not delete a valid counterexample.

Until those gates are met, report extension results in a validation queue: completed experiment, hypothesis, or unknown — not as upgraded core findings.

## How to use this page

- Landscape or audit tasks stop at the core 11 steps.
- Decision-validation tasks may *add* one bounded experiment from this page, labeled as such.
- Do not rename an unvalidated extension “the standard method” in a public report.
